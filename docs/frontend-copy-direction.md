# Frontend Copy Direction

This document is the master copy direction for all frontend expansion across the Zikel website and related product-facing marketing surfaces.

Use this as the default reference when writing or updating:

1. Page headlines
2. Subcopy
3. CTA language
4. Feature descriptions
5. Section intros
6. Trust and security messaging
7. Product explanation copy
8. Role-specific messaging

## Core Narrative

Zikel is a secure, multi-tenant care operations platform for children's homes that brings together day-to-day coordination, safeguarding intelligence, and regulatory evidence in one system.

Every major copy surface should reinforce this outcome:

1. Less fragmentation
2. Better safeguarding visibility
3. Faster compliance readiness
4. Clearer team accountability

## Who We’re Speaking To

Write primarily for:

1. Care home owners and directors
2. Responsible Individuals (RIs)
3. Home managers and deputies
4. Senior staff and operational leads
5. Compliance and safeguarding leads

## What Problems We Solve

These problems should be reinforced consistently:

1. Important records are scattered across tools and chats
2. Safeguarding signals are hard to spot early
3. Approval and sign-off workflows are inconsistent
4. Reg 44 / Reg 45 preparation is manual and stressful
5. Teams need audit-ready accountability without extra admin burden

## Product Story Pillars

New copy should expand naturally around these pillars:

1. Operational control across tenants, care groups, homes, staff, young people, vehicles, tasks, forms, logs, documents, and schedules
2. Safeguarding intelligence through chronology building, incident pattern detection, risk alert workflows, and reflective prompts
3. Compliance and reporting through evidence packs, RI dashboards, exports, and traceable provenance
4. Security and governance through role-based access, MFA-aware privileged actions, tenant isolation, signed webhooks, and auditable events
5. Team coordination through announcements, notifications, approvals, comments, and ownership tracking
6. Extensibility through integrations, webhook endpoints, delivery logs, and secure inbound/outbound event handling

## AI Copy Direction

AI should be positioned as guided support, not authority.

Always communicate that:

1. It helps teams prioritize, summarize, and identify risk signals
2. It uses safeguarding-conscious, non-blaming language guardrails
3. It supports reflective practice and action planning
4. It does not replace professional judgement, legal judgement, or clinical judgement

## Trust Language To Reuse

Favor language themes like:

1. Evidence-linked
2. Audit-ready
3. Tenant-scoped
4. Role-aware
5. Safeguarding-first
6. Built for accountable care operations

## Terminology Standards

Always use:

1. Young People
2. Homes
3. Care Groups
4. Tasks
5. Daily Logs
6. Safeguarding
7. Reg 44 / Reg 45
8. Evidence Pack
9. RI Dashboard
10. Tenant

Avoid:

1. Overclaim language like "instant compliance"
2. "Magic AI" phrasing
3. Clinical or legal certainty claims
4. Terms that conflict with care-sector context

## Tone And Style

All copy should be:

1. Professional, calm, practical, and credible
2. Clear and specific, never vague or hypey
3. Outcome-led, with operational realism
4. Safeguarding-sensitive and non-blaming
5. Confident without exaggeration

## Claim Guardrails

Only make claims we can defend from backend capability.

Allowed claim directions:

1. Structured workflows, audit trails, and evidence exports
2. Risk detection support, not guaranteed prevention
3. Security controls and governance features, not absolute security
4. AI-assisted guidance, not autonomous decision-making

## Copy Expansion Rule

Every new page, section, or major copy block should answer these four questions:

1. What operational, safeguarding, or compliance problem exists?
2. What outcome does Zikel create?
3. What backend capability makes that outcome real?
4. What action should the user take next?

## Working Rule

Treat this file as the source of truth for frontend copy direction unless it is explicitly replaced by a newer approved strategy note.
